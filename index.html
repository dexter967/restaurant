OUR STORY

An unhurried ceremony of spices & hospitality

Every custom seasoning mixture passing through our stone hearth mortar brings forward centuries of trade, local cultivation, and family secrets. Heavily inspired by old rain-washed courtyard mansions of North Kerala, our space balances heritage with modern stillness.
