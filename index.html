Our Story

A table set where
tradition meets taste

Nestled in the soul of Kozhikode, we are more than a place to eat — we are a memory waiting to be made. Born from a deep love of Malabar's culinary heritage, our kitchen breathes life into recipes passed down through generations. Every dish is a quiet conversation between past and present.

Slow-cooked, unhurried, honest — we believe food is ceremony. From the first aroma of roasting spices drifting through the doorway to the last sip of warm Sulaimani at dusk, everything here is crafted to feel like home.

If home happened to taste extraordinary.

From Our Kitchen

Dishes that carry the weight
of generations

A taste of what awaits you inside

Signature 🍛

Malabar Biryani

Slow dum-cooked with Kaima rice, whole spices, and tender meat. Sealed until each grain is perfumed. A dish that has no hurry.

House Special 🫓

Rumali Roti

Paper-thin handkerchief bread tossed on a convex tawa — served piping hot with rich Malabar gravy. Deceptively simple. Endlessly satisfying.

Café Special ☕

Sulaimani Chai

Malabar black tea with lemon, cardamom, and the warm quietness of cloves. The drink that ends every good meal here.

Slow Cooked 🥘

Dal Gosht

Lentils cooked down with tender mutton pieces, whole spices, and a touch of ghee. The kind of food that feeds both body and spirit.

Street Soul 🍱

Chaats & Street Plates

Pani Puri, Bhel Puri, and seasonal chaat creations — the bright, tangy, playful side of our kitchen. Honest street food done with care.

Fresh Daily 🍋

Fresh Lime & Shakes

Pressed fresh every hour. Lime with ginger and rock salt, seasonal shakes, and chilled drinks brewed slow — the pause your afternoon deserves.
